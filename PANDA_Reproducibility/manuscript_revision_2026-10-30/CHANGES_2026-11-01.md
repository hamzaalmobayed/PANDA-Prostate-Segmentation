## 2. V8. SICAPv2 scored region — done

- **Split of the unscored reference.** `sicap_scored_region.py` is extended; no model is needed. `SICAP_scored_region.csv` keeps its earlier columns unchanged and adds the split.
  - Test partition: 26.0% of all reference tumor pixels are unscored and not bright, as you computed.
  - Of these, 44.8% have saturation ≤ 0.20 (the fixed floor), 27.7% lie between 0.20 and the Otsu threshold of their patch, and 27.6% lie above the threshold and are removed by the morphological cleanup. No pixel comes from a tile on which a fallback of the rule applied.
  - Saturation medians: scored reference 92, unscored not-bright reference 54 (94 and 58 on your tile).
  - All tiles: 31.9% unscored and not bright; split 50.4%, 24.6% and 25.0%.
- **Dice under two other rules.** New script `PANDA_Pipeline/sicapv2_scoring_rules.py`, with launcher `run_sicapv2_scoring_rules.py`.
  - The probabilities of the official run were not saved, so the 2,122 test-partition patches were predicted again: same checkpoint, Vahadane target, threshold 0.43 and constant coordinate; 10 minutes on the GPU.
  - Only the test partition was re-run, because the value of the Abstract and l.800 is the test-partition one. The all-tile run was not repeated.
  - **Reproduction.** Every patch has the same scored pixels as in the official run, and 0.01% of the scored pixels changed class (non-determinism of the Vahadane fit and the GPU). Dice is 0.6535 over patches and 0.6277 over slide regions, as reported.
